# Problem Statement

## 1. Title

AlumniConnect – Alumni Networking & Career Engagement Platform

## 2. Domain

Education Technology / Alumni Management / Professional Networking / Career Development

## 3. Who is the user?

- Student: Creates a profile, manages education and skills, searches for alumni, connects with alumni, requests mentorship, explores career opportunities, and participates in alumni events.

- Alumni: Creates and manages a professional profile, connects with students and other alumni, provides career guidance, shares job opportunities, and participates in alumni events.

- Admin: Manages students, alumni, events, opportunities, connections, mentorship requests, and overall platform activities.

## 4. What problem are we solving?

Students often find it difficult to connect with alumni from their college who are working in different companies, industries, and locations. Alumni information may be distributed across college records, social media platforms, messaging groups, and personal contacts, making professional networking difficult and unorganized.

Students also lack a centralized platform to discover alumni, seek career guidance, learn about job opportunities, and participate in alumni events. AlumniConnect provides a centralized platform that connects students and alumni for professional networking, career guidance, opportunity sharing, mentorship, and alumni community activities.

## 7. User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| Student | Manage profile, education and skills, search alumni, send connection requests, request mentorship, view opportunities, and register for events |
| Alumni | Manage professional profile, skills and experience, connect with students and alumni, provide mentorship, post opportunities, and participate in events |
| Admin | Manage users, verify alumni, manage departments, events and opportunities, monitor connections, view reports, and manage platform activities |

## 8. Success Criteria

- User can register and log in.
- Student can create and complete a professional profile.
- Alumni can create and complete a professional profile.
- Alumni can add education, skills, experience, company, and location details.
- Student can search and filter alumni.
- Student can send a connection request to an alumni.
- Alumni can accept or reject connection requests.
- Student can send mentorship requests.
- Alumni can accept or manage mentorship requests.
- Alumni can post job and internship opportunities.
- Students can search and filter opportunities.
- Students can apply for suitable opportunities.
- Admin can verify and manage alumni accounts.
- Admin can manage users and platform activities.
- Users receive notifications for important platform activities.
- Students can discover and register for alumni events.
- Admin or alumni can create and manage events.
- System can provide relevant alumni and career recommendations.
